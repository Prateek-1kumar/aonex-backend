About the Google Pixel 10 5G

The Google Pixel 10 5G is engineered to be the most helpful Pixel yet. With the new Google Tensor G5 chip and a refined Pro-grade triple camera system, it captures stunning photos and 8K video in any light. The 6.3-inch Actua display refreshes at 120Hz for buttery-smooth scrolling and gaming, while the 12GB of RAM keeps every app responsive — even when juggling Gemini Nano workloads on-device.

With 256GB of storage, you have room for thousands of photos, hundreds of videos, and all your apps. The Obsidian finish features a matte aluminium frame and a polished glass back that resists fingerprints. IP68 rating means it shrugs off dust and survives accidental dunks.

Pixel 10 ships with Android 15 and seven years of OS, security, and Pixel Drop updates direct from Google. Built-in AI features include Magic Editor, Best Take, Audio Magic Eraser, Call Screen, Hold for Me, and Live Translate across 50+ languages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the Pixel 10 5G support eSIM?

A: Yes, the Pixel 10 5G supports both Nano SIM and eSIM, including dual-SIM dual-standby configurations.

Q: How long will it receive software updates?

A: Google guarantees seven years of OS, security, and Pixel feature drops, ending in late 2032.

Q: Is the charger included in the box?

A: No, the box contains the handset, USB-C cable, SIM ejector tool, and quick start guide only. A 30W or higher USB-C PD charger is recommended.

Q: What colours are available?

A: The Pixel 10 5G (12GB / 256GB) is available in Obsidian, Porcelain, Bay, and Limoncello.
