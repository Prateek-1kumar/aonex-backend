About the Google Pixel 8 5G

The Google Pixel 8 5G delivers a thoughtful blend of hardware and AI software. The new Google Tensor G3 chip drives on-device Gemini Nano workloads while keeping the phone cool under sustained loads. The 6.2-inch Actua display delivers vibrant colours, 2000 nits of peak brightness, and a buttery-smooth 120Hz refresh rate for scrolling, video, and gaming.

With 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage in the calming Hazel finish, the Pixel 8 keeps everyday apps responsive and has room for thousands of photos and your favourite videos. The IP68 rating means it shrugs off dust and short dunks in water, so a sudden monsoon downpour is no problem.

Pixel 8 ships with Android 14 and seven years of OS, security, and Pixel Drop updates direct from Google. Built-in AI features include Magic Editor, Best Take, Audio Magic Eraser, Call Screen, Hold for Me, and Live Translate across 50+ languages.
