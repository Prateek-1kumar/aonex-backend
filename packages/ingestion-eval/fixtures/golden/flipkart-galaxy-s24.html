About the Samsung Galaxy S24 5G

The Samsung Galaxy S24 5G ushers in the Galaxy AI era with a suite of on-device intelligence features powered by the Exynos 2400 chip. Live Translate handles phone calls in real time, Circle to Search lets you visually look up anything on screen with a long-press, and Generative Edits lets you reframe and reimagine photos right inside the Gallery.

The 6.2-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X panel pushes a buttery 120Hz refresh rate with 2,600 nits of peak brightness for legibility even under harsh sunlight. The triple camera system pairs a 50MP main shooter with a 12MP ultrawide and a 10MP 3x optical telephoto, delivering versatile shooting at any focal length.

Galaxy S24 ships with Android 14 and One UI 6.1 layered on top, and Samsung is committed to seven years of major Android upgrades and security updates. IP68 dust and water resistance, Gorilla Glass Victus 2 on the front and back, and an aluminium frame round out the durability story.

Q: Does the Galaxy S24 support eSIM in India?

A: Yes, the Indian Galaxy S24 supports both Nano SIM and eSIM, enabling dual-SIM dual-standby with one physical and one digital SIM.

A: Samsung guarantees seven years of major Android upgrades and security updates, taking the Galaxy S24 through 2031.

Q: Is a charger included in the box?

A: No, the box contains the handset, USB-C cable, ejector pin, and user manual only. A 25W or higher USB-C PD adapter is recommended for fast charging.

Q: Is the storage expandable?

A: No, the Galaxy S24 does not support microSD card expansion. The 256GB variant offers ample storage for photos, videos, and apps.
