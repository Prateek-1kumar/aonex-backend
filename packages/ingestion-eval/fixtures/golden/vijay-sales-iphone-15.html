About the Apple iPhone 15

The Apple iPhone 15 brings the Dynamic Island and a 48MP main camera to the standard iPhone for the first time, alongside a sleek aluminium and colour-infused glass design. The A16 Bionic chip powers smooth multitasking and console-quality gaming, while iOS 17 adds StandBy mode, customisable Contact Posters, and live voicemail.

With 128GB of storage in the Black finish, the iPhone 15 offers ample room for photos, videos, and apps. The new USB-C port replaces Lightning across the lineup, enabling charging and data transfer with the same cable you use for iPad and Mac.

iPhone 15 ships with iOS 17 and Apple promises five years of major software updates. Built-in safety features include Crash Detection, Emergency SOS via satellite, and Roadside Assistance via satellite (subject to availability in your region).
